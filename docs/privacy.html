Privacy policy

Miyoo Friends Library accesses your private shared library to let you browse and download selected files on your own device.

Google data accessed and purpose

With your consent, rclone uses Google Drive's read-only scope to read folder names, file names, file identifiers, sizes, modification information and available hashes, and to download selected file content. This data is used to display the library, identify existing downloads, and check transfer completion.

The app uses a configured shared folder ID to limit its browsing. The OAuth scope itself can access other files your account is permitted to read. It does not authorize uploads, deletion, or changes to Drive sharing.

Storage on your computer and microSD card

The setup helper writes your individual OAuth configuration, including access and refresh tokens, to .config/friends-library/rclone.conf on the selected card. Temporary configuration files are used during setup. Library settings, cached listings and installation records are also stored locally. Downloaded files are stored in OnionOS's ROM directories.

Desktop OAuth application identifiers in the installation package identify the app. They are separate from each user's account tokens. No shared master account token is distributed.

Transmission and sharing

The application and setup helper communicate directly with Google through rclone for authorization, token refresh, listing and downloads. The app does not send your account tokens, library listings or downloaded games to a custom backend, advertising service or analytics provider. Its normal operation does not transmit those files to the package distributor.

Your Google data is not sold or used for advertising, profiling, or AI model training. Miyoo Friends Library's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention, deletion and account disconnection

Cached listings and installation records remain on your card until refreshed or removed. Account credentials remain until you disconnect or remove them. Use the app's account-disconnection screen to delete local credentials and attempt online revocation. You can also revoke the app's access in your Google account's third-party connections settings.

Disconnection or uninstalling the application does not remove downloaded ROMs. Removing the app folder alone does not delete the separate account configuration directory. Delete that app-specific configuration directory yourself if you want to remove its remaining local settings and cache.

Security and your choices

Google requests use HTTPS. Tokens are not intentionally printed in application logs. FAT32 cards cannot enforce reliable per-file privacy permissions: anyone with physical access to your card may be able to read your credentials. Protect the card, disconnect before sharing it, and revoke access if it is lost. Do not send your rclone.conf file to other participants.

Google may expire or revoke authorization; reconnecting then requires a computer. You may decline authorization, in which case private Drive browsing and downloads are unavailable.

This informational website

These static pages contain no sign-in form, analytics script, advertising, or application credential collection. The website is intended for GitHub Pages hosting. GitHub may process ordinary web request information such as IP addresses and access logs under its privacy statement.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the friend who distributed the application or use the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
