FRIENDS LIBRARY - FIRST SETUP

OPTIONAL PHONE SETUP (first login or reconnect, v0.3.1)
After copying App to the SD card, open Friends Library > A: Connect
or SELECT: Account > Connect with phone. Help contains setup instructions. You can sign in using a phone instead
of a computer, without taking the card out again. The configured HTTPS QR
opens in Safari/Chrome; no extra phone app or SSH is needed. Compare the
six-digit code, approve read-only Google access, then press A on the Miyoo
to save the connection. Authorization and renewal use the free HTTPS service;
ROMs still download directly from Google. Read docs/HTTPS_SETUP.html in the
app folder. Real Android/iOS + Miyoo testing is still pending. SSH settings are not part of the app menu. Computer setup below remains available unchanged.

Read the accompanying Turkish PDF guide before starting.
Extract the entire ZIP on your computer. Copy/merge only App to the SD root.
Keep FirstSetup on the computer.

Windows 10/11 x64: double-click Connect-Windows.cmd and enter the SD drive.
macOS 12+: open Terminal, type "bash " (with a space), drag Connect-Mac.command
into Terminal, then press Enter. Select the SD card root when prompted.

Sign in with YOUR Google account. It must be invited to the private Drive
folder. The shared app is now In production; no test-user entry is required.
No new OAuth client is needed.
Wait for READY in Terminal. Safely eject the card, then launch
Apps > Friends Library on your Miyoo Mini Plus (OnionOS 4.3).

This package has Desktop application metadata, NOT anyone's account tokens.
Do not share .config/friends-library from your card.
The app moved to In production on 14 November 2026. Reconnect once with YES
if you originally signed in during Testing. New Production authorizations
are not subject to the Testing-only seven-day expiry. Access can still be
revoked or expire for other reasons. Google verification remains incomplete;
an unverified-app warning and 100-user cap remain.

Windows setup has mock-response tests. Mac setup has a bash syntax check;
real Mac OAuth and end-to-end device download acceptance remain unverified.
